What it's for

Your hands already know the shortcuts. The apps just disagree.

Each app keeps its own shortcuts. Cat Key adds yours on top, everywhere or app by app, and switches off with one click (or a nap).

🍎

Switching from a Mac

Alt sits where ⌘ was. With Cat Key, Alt+C copies, Alt+W closes, Alt+Q quits, Alt+Shift+4 grabs part of the screen, everywhere. Moving to a Mac? It works the other way too.

🎬

Pros with many apps

Premiere keys inside DaVinci Resolve. Photoshop habits in other editors. One set of keys for the same job in every app you touch.

⌨️

Anyone tired of fighting shortcuts

Put any action on the keys you like. Open apps, type text, block the Insert key you keep hitting. One click to swap a shortcut around.

What's inside

A whole toolbox, in one friendly cat.

📚

Library

30 ready-made packs, 1,097 shortcuts: Mac ⇄ Windows switching and the pro shortcuts of the apps creators use. Scan my apps shows which of yours are covered, and gets the rest, even rare apps, from an AI.

🎯

Train yourself

A quick game that teaches an app's shortcuts, spaced out so they stick. A right answer goes boing; a miss gets a soft meow.

🪄

Cat Builder

Shortcuts for any app, or a whole new cat: Cat Key writes the request, opens ChatGPT or Claude (in your browser or their apps) with all of it in, and picks up the answer when you copy it.

🧑‍🏫

Shortcut coach

Click something that has a shortcut and a little note shows the keys, then fades. Ignore it for good, dismiss it for now, or adjust it into a shortcut of your own, and put the note anywhere on screen. It never looks at your screen: it reads only the name of what you clicked, like a screen reader.

🐱

Cat Dock

Your cat on the edge of the screen: drop files and text on it, drag them back out anywhere, see what you copied, run a focus timer, and watch it get serious when your computer works hard.

🤝

Claude hands it over

Connect Claude Desktop once, then ask it for shortcuts for any app. It looks up the real ones and sends the pack straight to Cat Key, where you pick what to add.

🔀

App switcher

Rest the pointer in a corner and your open windows pop up right there. Click one to jump to it. Faster than Alt+Tab, and it never takes the keyboard.

🐾

Paws

Paw prints in your cat's colours where you click and drag, gone in a second. On Windows, your pointer can be a cat paw too.

⚔️

Cat Warrior

From Kitten to Keyboard Warrior. XP for every shortcut, the time saved vs. the time the mouse costs, new gear for your cat at every rank, and a leaderboard.

⌨️

Touch typing

Home row first, a new key only when you're quick and accurate, lines built around your weakest key, and a no peeking mode.

🐈

Your cat, your look

Eleven cats with their own outfits (turbans, helmets, capes), or design your own with an AI. Cat sounds on every click, and a purr to work to.

🔗

Store & share

Post a pack from Cat Key: it shows with your GitHub name and people ❤️ it. Or send it as one line of text. Move your setup to a Mac; Cat Key translates ⌘ and Ctrl.

🩺

Checkup

Finds what gets in the way (other remappers, start-up, admin apps) and fixes it, or shows you where to click.

⚡

Fast and careful

Under a millisecond per key. Never misses a release, stays out of the way while you type, and keeps no keystroke log.

Train yourself

From "where was that menu?" to muscle memory.

  • You recall, not read. Pulling the keys from memory is what makes them stick.
  • Spaced review. Each shortcut returns right before you'd forget it, then less and less often.
  • Mixed, not drilled in blocks, with instant sound and a little picture of what the action does.

🍎 Opening Cat Key on a Mac the first time

Cat Key isn't notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks before it opens it. Drag Cat Key into Applications, open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.

If that goes round in circles, paste this into Terminal. It removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag from Cat Key only, and opens it:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Cat Key.app" && open "/Applications/Cat Key.app"

Then let Cat Key use the keyboard: when macOS asks, switch it on under Accessibility.
